# Cpp-game-project
## Game Concept
  Shadow Realm : dream weaver is a surreal dark fantasy - action survival game thar explores the fractured boundary between waking and dreaming .
  The player navigates a decaying gothic world reality shifts based on thier dream state .
## Visual Style & Theme 
  - **style:** low poly dark fantasy with faceted 3d geometry , reminiscent of 90s era retro game art.
  - **Lighting & Atmosphere:** Eerie atmosphere lighting paired with a distinct "dreamcore" aesthetic.
  - **Tone:** Surreal , atmospheric , and nostalgic .
  - ## Core Gameplay & Mechanics
  - **Dream Navigation:** The world dynamically shifts between dream states for example a solid village bridge in one moment might be collapsed in the next .
  - **Dream Anchors:** Players must find spiritual  anchors ( wolf , toad , eagle , or a crow ) to reset reality and stabilize the world .
  - **Ultimate Goal:** Navigate the " Dream Centre " and make thr final choice : wakeup or stay in the dream  forever .

 ## Game Engine  & Tools 
  - **Engine:** Unreal engine 5 / unity
  - **Language:** C++
  - **3D Assets & Modeling :** Blender
